A02.1 — Rohan: The Stamina Joke

Walking track · Ankita × Rohan

APPROVED

Gurgaon ki January subah mein Gulmohar Heights ka walking track halki dhund se dhaka tha. Ankita fitted athletic jacket, running tights aur trainers mein brisk pace maintain kar rahi thi; uske lambe baal ponytail mein bandhe the.

Rohan: Good morning, Mrs. Discipline! New Year pe bhi koi chhutti nahi?

Ankita: Good morning, Mr. Excuses! Tum aaj time par kaise aa gaye?

Rohan: Tumse inspire hokar. Waise aaj tumhara sporty look kaafi suit kar raha hai—jacket, ponytail, confident walk. Society functions wali Ankita se different.

Ankita: Achha? Matlab normally boring lagti hoon?

Rohan: Compliment diya hai, madam. Cross-examination mat shuru karo.

Ankita: IT Tech Lead hoon. Har statement verify karti hoon.

Rohan: Fine. Confident, stylish, aur dangerously good at arguing.

Ankita: Dangerously? Itna dramatic mat bano. Waise tumhari stamina improve hui hai. Pehle do rounds mein excuses shuru ho jaate the.

Rohan: Stamina toh ab kaafi improve ho gayi hai. Do rounds ke baad bhi energy bachti hai… bas partner mein utna enthusiasm hona chahiye.

Ankita: Rohan! Hum jogging ki baat kar rahe hain… na ki night ex—

Ankita ekdum ruk gayi. Apni zubaan halki si daanton ke beech daba kar phir neeche ka honth bite kiya. Oh God, Ankita! Yeh kya bol diya? Usne nazar track ki taraf kar li.

Rohan: Night exercise? Maine toh morning stamina ki baat ki thi. Raat tumhare dimaag mein kaise aa gayi?

Ankita: Oh, shut up, Rohan!

Rohan: Morning ho ya night, tum hamesha itni competitive rehti ho?

Ankita: Tumhe har baat ka double meaning nikalna zaroori hai?

Rohan: Main? Tumne toh meri innocent baat ko completely different direction de di.

Ankita hans padi aur walk tez kar di. Baat mazaak mein rahi, lekin apni accidental slip ne use ek pal ke liye pleasantly fluster kar diya. Aage jogging track ke mod par Nikhil cooldown walk karta dikh gaya.

A02.2 — Nikhil Joins: The Jacket Suspense

Walking track · Ankita × Rohan × Nikhil

APPROVED

Ankita: Difference delivery ka hai, Rohan.

Nikhil: Compliment aur running commentary mein farq hota hai.

Nikhil: Meri choice alag hai—midnight-blue sleeveless performance top. Tumhari shoulder definition aur posture clearly dikhega. You've worked for that fitness.

Ankita: Gentlemen, appraisal complete. Jacket meri hai, suspense tum dono ka.

Nikhil: Well played, Ankita.

Rohan: Okay, tum jeet gayi. Lekin next Monday ke step-count challenge ko bhoolna mat.

Jacket meri hai, suspense tum dono ka.

Walking track se exercise corner tak aate hue Ankita ko apna hi jawab yaad aaya. Woh muskurayi.

Well, Rohan ki choice toh coincidence se already match kar gayi.

Woh jacket bench par rakhkar stretch karne lagi. Vivek ne uske shoulder alignment ko dekha.

Vivek: Waise progress clearly dikhti hai. Shoulders ka shape stronger hua hai, waist aur core control bhi improve hua hai. Regular training ka result hai.

06:43 AM

Vivek uske saamne khada hua.

Vivek: Chest opener. Feet shoulder-width apart. Arms side mein kholo, inhale karte hue chest lift karo, exhale par arms centre mein lao. Six slow repetitions.

Ankita ne demonstration follow kiya.

Vivek: Right shoulder thoda elevated hai. Elbow angle dikha doon?

Ankita: Haan, dikhao. Phir main khud maintain kar lungi.

Vivek kareeb aaya. Uski ungliyan Ankita ke right shoulder par halki si tikin, phir usne forearm ka angle adjust kiya.

Vivek: Yahan relax. Ab elbow thoda upar. Perfect. Hold.

Thandi subah mein uske haath ki warmth Ankita ko unexpectedly noticeable lagi.

Wahi shoulder… jiski Nikhil abhi tareef kar raha tha.

Woh ek second ke liye apne movement se zyada Vivek ki proximity ke baare mein conscious ho gayi.

Vivek: Ankita? Breathing.

Ankita: Haan! Sorry. Tumhari instructions follow kar rahi hoon.

Vivek: Sure? Kyunki tum usually third rep tak mujhe correct karna shuru kar deti ho.

Ankita: Don't tempt me. Abhi bhi kar sakti hoon.

Vivek peeche hatkar agli repetition demonstrate karne laga.

06:47 AM

Vikram, Shreya ka husband, cooldown ke liye exercise corner par aa gaya.

Vikram: Happy New Year! Tum dono itni subah bhi training kar rahe ho? Mujhe laga resolutions kal se tootne shuru honge.

Ankita: Happy New Year, Vikram. Aapko toh attendance register mein late mark karna chahiye.

Vikram: Madam, main toh bas dekhne aaya tha ki Vivek ka chest-opener workout kya kamaal karta hai.

Vivek: Perfect timing. Tum bhi join karo. Arms open, chest up, shoulders back.

Dono ne movement shuru kiya. Inhale ke saath Ankita ka chest naturally utha aur fitted tee uski upper-body movement ke saath shift hui. Vikram ki nazar ek pal wahan gayi, phir usne teasing smile ke saath Vivek ko dekha.

Vikram: Vivek, ek baat bata. Is exercise mein breath control mushkil hai ya attention control?

Ankita ne uski nazar aur joke dono pakad liye.

Ankita: Aapki breathing toh theek lag rahi hai. Attention ki problem serious hai kya?

Vikram: Main toh exercise ki baat kar raha tha!

Ankita: Aur main bhi. Ya phir aap kuch aur explain karna chahte hain?

Vivek hans diya.

Vivek: Vikram, pehle six reps complete karo. Tumhari counting toh pehle hi ruk gayi.

Vikram: Kya karun? Tumhare sessions mein concentration test bhi surprise mein milta hai.

Ankita: Shreya ko bataungi New Year ke first day aapka concentration fail ho gaya.

Vikram: Shreya ko mat batana. Woh toh poora interrogation shuru kar degi—kis taraf dekh rahe the, kyun dekh rahe the!

Vivek: Toh simple solution. Mere demonstration par focus karo.

Ankita: Exactly. Meri fitness progress dekhne ka subscription free nahi hai, gentlemen.

Vikram zor se hans pada.

Vikram: Fair enough! Waise genuinely, Ankita, tumhari shoulder definition aur overall figure mein noticeable difference hai. Jacket mein itna clear nahi hota.

Ankita ne apni ponytail theek ki.

Ankita: Thank you. Dekha, Vivek? Jab concentration wapas aati hai toh compliment bhi dhang se nikalta hai.

Ankita: Vivek, ek professional question. Fitted tee better hai ya sleeveless performance top?

Midnight-blue sleeveless… Nikhil ne exactly wahi suggest kiya tha.

Vikram: Lagta hai next session mein wardrobe surprise milega.

Ankita: Surprise ki guarantee nahi. Lekin tum logon ki curiosity kaafi entertaining hai.

Vivek: Next time shoulder alignment phir check karenge. Aaj ka progress achha tha.

Ankita: Deal. Aur Vikram, next time counting practice karke aana.

Ankita exercise corner se ghar ki taraf chal di.

Rohan ki teasing, Nikhil ka measured compliment, Vivek ka shoulder correction aur Vikram ki mischievous nazar—sab alag the.

Usne ek baar apni jacket ko dekha.

Funny. Pehle main bas workout karti thi. Aaj main khud bhi notice kar rahi hoon ki kaun kya notice karta hai.

Woh halki si muskurayi. Ab breakfast ke liye ghar jaana tha.
