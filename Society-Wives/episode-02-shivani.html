Shivani — Three Men, One Private Photograph

Approved chronological plot · Thursday, 1 January 2026 · Gurgaon · One shared society and college clock

Story brief

Shivani, a 31-year-old college lecturer, plans a private New Year surprise for Rohan, her husband. An urgent call interrupts it. A private, non-nude photograph remains saved in her phone's local gallery. Later, the ordinary setup for a college presentation becomes the episode's turning point when the image is exposed and three men independently choose to retain copies without her consent.

Their professional roles and private reactions are different. Shivani remains unaware. This episode ends with her still at college at about 13:20–13:30.
