Use this flavor for future dialogues

Sexual innuendo / double entendre / suggestive teasing can be playful and grown-up without turning each encounter into an affair. Build a natural remark, a cheeky alternative meaning, a momentary response, then return the character to ordinary life.

Different voices: Rohan is brazen and funny; Nikhil is dry, observant and unexpectedly bold; Ankita is self-possessed, briefly flustered, and then capable of flipping the joke back on them.

Both Rohan and Nikhil are married men. This is a social interaction on a cold Gurgaon morning: no jacket removal, physical contact, sudden outfit change, or new off-screen rumor.

Continuity safeguards

Keep jokes about hypothetical gossip distinct from real circulated rumors. Compliments need not be identical or reciprocated. Married status, established relationships, separate viewpoints, consent, specific clothing, time and location remain fixed by the master plot.

For future scenes, the writer can intensify wit or innuendo only when plot and character behavior support it. A remembered line is a seed, not permission to invent later events.
