### Beat 1 — She dresses for him

*Morning / 4:25 PM*

Wednesday subah Ankita ne burgundy dress haath mein lekar mirror ke saamne dekha.

*Friday ko Rohit meri taraf dekhkar apni baat bhool gaya tha. Aaj kya karega?*

*4:35 PM · AV desk*

Rohit ne monitor se nazar uthayi.

“Good afternoon, ma'am.”

Phir woh ruk gaya.

Rohit ki nazar ek pal uske outfit par thahri. Jab Ankita counter ke paas aakar slightly turned hui, usne side slit aur dress ki elegant silhouette bhi notice ki. Phir uski nazar Ankita ke chehre par laut aayi.

**Ankita ne poora reaction notice kar liya.**

“Rohit Babu?”

“Ji?”

“Tum mujhe dekh rahe the ya adapter?”

Rohit ne muskurakar kaha, “Dono. Lekin adapter ka test complete hai.”

“Achha? Aur doosra inspection?”

“Uska result bol doon?”

“Permission granted.”

Rohit ne seedha uski aankhon mein dekha.

“Tum bahut achhi lag rahi ho, Ankita.”

Ankita ke chehre par dheere se smile aayi.

*Finally. Friday ko jo bol nahi paaya tha, aaj khud bol diya.*
