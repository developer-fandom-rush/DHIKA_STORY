**ANKITA · EPISODE 03 · SCENE 02**  
**The Five-Person Stamina Challenge**  
**1 January 2026 · 13:15–13:45 · Cyber City IT Office**

01:15 PM. Release-monitoring screens par koi naya alert nahi tha. Team ne thodi der ke liye desks se hatkar open collaboration area mein break liya tha. Whiteboard ke paas portable speaker, ek practice mat aur event backdrop stand rakha tha—Aishwarya ne celebration ki tayari ke liye wahin chhoda tha.

Sana aur Aishwarya saath mein aayi. Dono late thi. Sana ne black fitted dress pehna tha, Aishwarya ne deep maroon blouse aur skirt. Jackets unke haath mein thi.

**Vivan:** “Aishwarya ma’am, aap HR ho aur khud late? Attendance rules kahan gaye?”

**Aishwarya:** “Rules tab kaam aate hain jab koi surprise prepare na kar raha ho.”

**Vivan:** “Toh surprise ke naam pe late entry maaf ho jayegi?”

**Aishwarya:** “Pehle surprise dekh lena. Phir complaint file kar dena.”

Ankita ne unhe dekha aur hasi.

**Sana:** “Hum teen aur tum dono. Sach mein itna stamina hai ki handle kar loge?”

Woh Ankita aur Aishwarya ke paas aa gayi.

**Vivan:** “Teen versus two? Yeh unfair hai.”

**Aishwarya:** “HR warning — overconfidence ka insurance nahi milta.”

**Ankita:** “Tum dono planning bahut karte ho. Ab execution dekhte hain.”

**Reyansh:** “Main ready hoon. Bas blame game mat shuru kar dena.”

**Reyansh:** “Fine. Late entry ki penalty: ek chhota dance demo tum dono lead karogi.”

**Sana:** “Done. Lekin phir hum teenon ko judge karne mat baith jaana.”

**Ankita:** “Main judge nahi, participant hoon. Step easy hua toh.”

---

Sana ne jacket utaari. Aishwarya ne bhi. Dono ne chhota sa dance step dikhaya. Ankita bhi join ho gayi kyunki usse maza aa raha tha. Kurti uske body pe move ho rahi thi.

Sana ne tez, chhote steps pakde; Aishwarya apni rhythm par relaxed thi. Ankita unke beech aa gayi toh teeno ki timing ek pal ko bigdi, phir sab ek saath hans pade. Reyansh ne speaker ka volume halka sa kam kiya.

Vivan ne Ankita ki chaal dekhi. Rounded hips, apple-like silhouette clearly dikh raha tha.

**Vivan:** “Ankita ma’am, aaj walk mein bhi promotion lag raha hai.”

Ankita ne turn liya. Reyansh ki nazar uspe ruki. Usne pakad liya.

**Ankita:** “Reyansh, tumhari nazar bhi appraisal kar rahi hai kya?”

**Reyansh:** “Sirf observe kar raha hoon.”

---

Next turn mein Ankita ne do kadam peeche liye. Vivan uske baayein tha, Reyansh whiteboard ki taraf. Mat ka kona backdrop stand ke base se laga hua tha; kisi ne music ke beech us par dhyaan nahi diya.

Dance ke dauran Ankita ka pair backdrop stand pe atka. Woh jhuki. Vivan ne uski waist pakad ke support kiya. Reyansh ne haath badhaya. Uska haath Ankita ke top ke loose edge ke neeche chala gaya, stomach pe firm five-finger grip. Woh tab tak nahi hataya jab tak Ankita seedhi nahi ho gayi.

Ankita ne feel kiya. Closeness clear thi. Jab balance aa gaya, usne Reyansh ki taraf dekha.

**Ankita:** “Reyansh, tumhara grip toh kaafi strong hai.”

**Reyansh:** “Safety first.”

---

Music rukte hi Sana ne aankhein gol karke dono boys ki taraf dekha, jaise ab woh rescue ka score announce karne wali ho. Ankita ne pehle apni kurti theek ki, phir khud hi hans di. Baat ko lamba kheenchne ke bajay group ka mood phir apni purani masti mein aa gaya.

Sab wapas banter pe aaye.

**Vivan:** “Waise ma’am, subah Priya aur Nisha ne aapki growth report banayi thi. Final appraisal hua?”

**Ankita:** “Tum log meri personal appraisal kab se karne lage?”

**Aishwarya:** “Kaisi growth? Main HR hoon, mujhe kyun nahi bataya?”

**Sana:** “Shaadi ke baad figure mein jo promotions hue hain. Results impressive hain.”

**Reyansh:** “Congratulations bolun ya performance bonus?”

Ankita hasi, phir bol padi.

**Ankita:** “Arre, Kabir ko toh aaj tak koi complaint nahi hui!”

Sab ruk gaye. Phir has pade.

**Vivan:** “Humne toh complaint poochhi hi nahi!”

**Aishwarya:** “Yeh information tumne khud disclose ki hai!”

**Sana:** “Ab double meaning explain bhi khud karna padega!”

Ankita ke gaal garam ho gaye.

**Ankita:** “Mera woh matlab nahi tha!”

**Reyansh:** “Humne kuch nahi bola. Aapne khud report submit ki.”

Ankita abhi Reyansh ko jawab dene ke liye mud rahi thi. Sana ne Aishwarya ko shararti sa look diya aur chupke se uske peeche pahunch gayi.

Sana peeche se aayi. Ek haath se Ankita ki bum pinch ki, doosre se breast. Ankita ka body ek second ke liye hil gaya. Vivan aur Reyansh ki aankhein badi ho gayi. Sana has ke bhaag gayi.

**Sana:** “Promotion ki checking bhi toh banti hai!”

**Ankita:** “SANAAA! Ruk!”

Sana whiteboard ke paas se ghoomi, hans kar peeche dekha. Ankita ne bhi bina ruke uska peechha kiya.

Ankita uske peeche bhaagi. Sana ka pair mat pe atka. Dono sofa pe gir padi. Hasne lagi. Office mein ab sirf unki hansi thi.

**Aishwarya:** “Excellent. HR report mein likhungi—two employees failed the stability test.”

**Reyansh:** “Crash test pass ho gaya, at least.”

**Vivan:** “Bas ab next round whiteboard pe. Floor ko aur targets mat dena.”

Ankita sofa se uthte hue Sana ko dekhkar phir hansi. Sana ne haath utha kar haar maan li. Aishwarya ne speaker band kiya aur paanchon whiteboard ke paas laut aaye. Party ka surprise abhi bhi un dono ne reveal nahi kiya tha.
