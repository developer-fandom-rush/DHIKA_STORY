F01 — Two Women, Two Men

AUTHOR APPROVED · PUBLISHED · 1 January 2026 · Approximately 5:00 PM · Hospital staff exit

Hospital staff exit ke bahar shaam ki halki roshni mein chaaron log ikatthe hue. Aditi white coat ke neeche fitted black shirt aur straight trousers mein khadi thi, baal loosely tied, posture seedha aur nazar confidently aage. Avi dark shirt ke sleeves roll karke uske saamne aaya. Yash thodi doori pe khada tha, expression relaxed. Meenakshi plum kurta, cream palazzo aur grey cardigan mein unke paas pahunchi, keys haath mein.

Aditi: “Avi, hospital mein toh bade disciplined rehte ho. Aaj dance floor pe meri pace match kar paoge?”

Avi ne uski nazar se nazar milayi. Woh normal baat expect kar raha tha, lekin Aditi ka tone seedha aur testing tha.

Avi: “Tum pace badhaogi toh mujhe bhi apna best dena padega.”

Aditi: “Best dena. Excuses mat dena. Main performance yaad rakhti hoon.”

Avi jawab dene wala tha, lekin Aditi ke steady eye contact ne use ek pal ke liye rok diya. Meenakshi ne uska badalta expression notice kiya.

Yash: “Meenakshi ma'am, lagta hai Avi ko aaj extra training chahiye.”

Meenakshi: “Training se zyada concentration. Aditi abhi warm-up mein hai aur yeh already nervous hai.”

Aditi hans padi. Avi ne Meenakshi ki taraf dekha—ab use samajh aa gaya tha ki aaj teasing sirf ek taraf se nahi hone wali.

Aditi: “Avi, tumhari aankhon mein confidence toh hai. Bas dance floor pe meri aankhon se nazar mat churana.”

Avi: “Tum saamne rahogi toh nazar hatane ka sawaal hi nahi.”

Aditi ki smile gehri hui. Is baar Avi ne jawab dene mein der nahi lagayi.

Yash: “Aditi, tum toh already floor set kar rahi ho. Hum log abhi sirf audience hain.”

Aditi: “Audience nahi, Yash. Participants.”

Meenakshi ne keys uthayi aur group ki taraf ishara kiya.

Meenakshi: “Chalo. Parking door hai. Zyada der mat lagao.”

Aditi parking ki taraf badhi. Avi uske saath chalne laga. Is baar Aditi ne khud uski taraf dekha aur muskura di. Meenakshi ne woh exchange notice kiya, lekin kuch kaha nahi. Chaaron parking ki taraf badh gaye.

Cut: All four walk toward parking. Car ride and seating are F02, not yet written.

F04 — Aditi Sets the Temperature

Meenakshi: “Aditi, hospital wali tumhe pehchanna mushkil ho raha hai. Dress genuinely stunning hai.”

Music ki beat badli. Aditi ne apna glass table par rakha aur dance floor ki taraf badhi. Pehle usne akele kuch confident steps liye. Ek turn ke saath uski burgundy dress ki short hemline halki si move hui. Avi aur Yash dono usse dekh rahe the.

F06 — Yash Joins

AUTHOR-REQUESTED WORKING PUBLICATION · NOT FINAL APPROVED · Farmhouse evening

Aditi ne Yash ki taraf haath badhaya. Woh abhi bhi Avi ke dance se thodi saans le rahi thi, burgundy dress uske body se chipka hua tha.

Aditi: “Yash. Tum bhi aa jao.”

Yash ne glass side pe rakha aur aage badha. Usne kuch nahi kaha. Seedha Aditi ke peeche aa gaya. Avi abhi bhi saamne tha. Aditi beech mein sandwich ho gayi.

Aditi ne palatkar Yash ko dekha. Avi ke saath har step pe baat hoti rahi thi; Yash ki khamoshi mein kuch alag tha. Usne abhi tak ek bhi compliment nahi diya tha, phir bhi uski entry ne dance ka rhythm badal diya.

Aditi: “Itni der chup the. Ab bina warning ke lead lene aa gaye?”

Yash: “Tum dono ka dance dekh raha tha. Kab bolna hai, usse zyada zaroori tha kab aana hai.”

Yash ne Aditi ki taraf apna haath badhaya. Aditi ne khud use thaama. Agli beat par usne unhe ek saaf turn mein guide kiya; Avi ek step hatkar unke saamne aa gaya. Aditi ko surprise hua ki Yash music mein itna sure-footed hai, aur is baar woh khushi se uski lead follow karne lagi.

Aditi: “Hospital mein toh tum itne quiet rehte ho.”

Yash: “Wahan tum meri senior ho. Yahan tumne khud dance ke liye bulaya hai.”

Aditi ki hansi nikli. Avi ne bhi muskurane ki koshish ki, lekin usne pehli baar notice kiya ki Yash bina badi-badi baaton ke Aditi ko waqai surprise kar raha tha.

Avi: “Yash, tumne toh apna best move chhupa kar rakha tha.”

Yash: “Sab pehle hi dikha doon toh surprise kis baat ka?”

Music thoda slow hua. Yash ne agle turn ke baad Aditi ko rukne ki jagah di. Woh uski taraf mudhi, ab curious thi ki yeh shaant sa trainee aur kya unexpected kar sakta hai.

Yash: “Ek chhoti si gustakhi poochh sakta hoon?”

Aditi: “Pehle batao kaisi.”

Yash ki nazar ek pal uske kandhe ki taraf gayi. Aditi ne baat samjhi aur halki si muskurahat ke saath sir hila diya.

Aditi: “Ek baar. Uske baad dance pe dhyan.”

Yash ne uske kandhe par ek halki kiss di aur turant thoda peeche ho gaya. Aditi ek pal ke liye still hui—surprise gesture se kam, Yash ke poochhne aur phir bilkul theek waqt par ruk jaane se zyada tha. Agli beat par woh khud uske saath turn mein aa gayi.

Aditi: “Achha, Avi. Tumne ise itna shaant samajh kaise liya?”

Avi: “Maine samjha tha yeh sirf dekhne aaya hai. Galat nikla.”

Meenakshi sofa se dekh rahi thi. Avi ke har emotion ka chehre par aana aasaan tha; Yash ne toh bolne se pehle poora dance dekh liya tha. Usne Aditi ki surprised smile aur Avi ki badli hui nazar, dono note kiye.

Aditi ne ek aur halka turn liya, phir dono men se thodi door hatkar Meenakshi ki taraf dekha. Uski muskurahat mein agle gaane ka naya khayal tha—abhi invitation nahi, bas ek nazar. Dance ka agla hissa ab kisi aur ko bhi involve kar sakta tha.
