**Beat 3: Aishwarya’s Confrontation Cliff (15:00–15:10)**

Ankita briefing room se bahar aayi. Darwaaze tak aate-aate usne beige blazer utaar kar haath mein le liya tha. Kurti ke upper do buttons khule hue the. Uska chehra thoda garam tha.

Aishwarya corridor mein khadi thi. Usne Ankita ko dekha aur ruk gayi.

**Aishwarya:** “Ankita… meeting kaisi rahi?”

**Ankita:** “Ho gayi. Sign-off mil gaya.”

Aishwarya ki nazar uske open buttons pe gayi.

**Aishwarya:** “Aur yeh? Buttons bhi sign-off ke saath khul gaye?”

Ankita ne turant buttons ki taraf haath badhaya, phir rok liya.

**Ankita:** “Garmi lag rahi thi.”

**Aishwarya:** “Garmi? Andar AC chal raha tha. Ankita, kya hua andar?”

Ankita ne nazar hata li. Uski saans thodi tez thi.

**Aishwarya:** “Ankita, seedha batao. Bhalla aur Sandeep ne kuch kaha?”

**Ankita:** “Bas… compliments. Zyada kuch nahi.”

**Aishwarya:** “Compliments se tum aise nahi nikalti. Tumhara face bata raha hai.”

Ankita ne blazer pehenne ko uthaya, phir use baazu par rakh liya. Usne apni awaaz sambhali.

**Aishwarya:** “Ankita—”

**Ankita:** “Baad mein baat karte hain.”

Woh aage badh gayi. Aishwarya uske peeche dekhti rahi. Corridor mein ab sirf Ankita ki tez chaal ki awaaz thi.
