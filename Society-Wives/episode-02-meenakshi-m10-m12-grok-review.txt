## M10 — Kunal Finds the Locker Open

12:36–12:50 PM · Staff locker area · New draft for review

12:36 PM. Changing wing ke outer corridor mein traffic kam tha. Staff lockers ka row ek side par tha, aur light ke neeche Meenakshi Kapoor ke naam wala locker thoda sa khula dikh raha tha.

Dr. Kunal Bedi ruk gaya. Itne saalon se hospital mein kaam karte hue usne lockers ko hamesha band dekha tha. Khaas kar Meenakshi ka—woh apni files ki tarah personal belongings bhi carefully arrange karti thi.

Usne naam ki plate dobara dekhi. Door ka latch poori tarah engage nahi hua tha. Kisi ne pehle hi locker khola tha; 09:05 par Meenakshi ne khud use lock kiya tha, lekin ab woh theek se band nahi tha.

Kunal (khud se): “Meenakshi apna locker aise khula chhod de? Unlikely.”

Uske paas seedha staff security ko batane ka raasta tha. Phir bhi usne apni taraf se check karne ka galat faisla kiya. Door ko thoda aur khola. Andar neatly folded morning clothes, gray cardigan aur bag ki personal side pocket nazar aayi.

Jo cheezein saamne rakhi thi, woh Kunal ko sirf utni door se bhi dikh sakti thi. Lekin usne ek folded layer hataakar neeche dekh liya.

Kunal locker ke seedha saamne khada tha, door ko apne left haath se thoda aur kholkar. Andar gray cardigan ke neeche bag ki side pocket aur ek chhoti pouch clearly dikh rahi thi. Usne right haath se cardigan ka ek fold uthaya, neeche ki layer check ki, phir use wapas rakh diya—lekin crease ab alag jagah ban gayi. Uska chehra tight ho gaya, lips press hue, aur woh ek pal ke liye ruka jaise soch raha ho ki ab kya kare.

Kuch personal items apni jagah se hil gaye. Meenakshi se permission lene ka sawaal hi nahi utha—woh wahan thi hi nahi.

Kunal (dheere se): “Yeh mujhe nahi dekhna chahiye tha.”

Corridor ke doosre end par cart ki awaaz sunai di. Door ko dheere se aadha band karke woh peeche hata; latch ab bhi fully click nahi hua tha.

Usne guard ko call nahi kiya. Na staff register mein note banaya. Usne dobara latch check nahi kiya. Door band sa lag raha tha, magar ab bhi secure nahi tha.

12:50 PM. Kunal corridor se nikal gaya. Use pata tha ki woh ek khule locker par rukkar uski owner ki privacy cross kar chuka hai. Meenakshi ko is baat ka koi andaaza nahi tha.

## M11 — Khurana Closes the Locker

12:51–13:05 PM · Hospital staff passage · New draft for review

12:51 PM. Chairman Dr. S. P. Khurana clinical wing ke internal passage se guzre. Staff lockers ke saamne pahunchkar unki nazar ek door par gayi, jo row ke baaki darwazon se kuch millimetres bahar nikla hua tha.

Unhone rukkar plate padhi: Dr. Meenakshi Kapoor.

Khurana (khud se): “Yahan latch ka issue hai ya kisi ne open chhod diya?”

Unhone ek ungli se door ko halki si push di. Woh aur khul gaya. Andar upar rakha cardigan neatly folded nahi tha; bag ki zipper bhi us angle par thi jis tarah jaldi mein chhodi gayi ho.

Khurana locker ke right side se aaye, door jo pehle se thoda bahar nikla hua tha use left haath se aur push kiya. Latch half-open position mein thi. Unhone cardigan ko dono haathon se uthakar thoda aur andar ki taraf set kiya, bag ko shelf ke peeche khisakaya, aur side mein sarki hui pouch ko wapas center ki taraf kheench diya. Har movement ke baad arrangement pehle se zyada disturbed dikh raha tha. Pouch asli jagah par nahi lauti; woh shelf ke left hisse mein reh gayi.

Unki nazar andar gayi, phir corridor ki taraf. Koi staff member paas nahi tha. Khurana samajh sakte the ki aise personal locker ki situation security ko report karni chahiye, lekin unhone kisi ko bulaya nahi.

Woh kuch pal us arrangement ko dekhte rahe, jaise khud se poochh rahe hon ki andar ki cheezon ko chhoona sahi faisla tha bhi ya nahi.

Ab jo cheezein pehle se displaced thi, unka original arrangement aur badal chuka tha. Khurana yeh nahi jaan sakte the ki andar pehle kisne haath lagaya tha. Unhone na inventory banayi, na koi witness bulaya.

Khurana: “Owner se hi check karwa lena chahiye.”

Lekin yeh baat sirf unhone apne aap se kahi. Na Meenakshi ko message gaya, na security desk ko.

13:05 PM. Unhone door ko dheere se pull karke band kiya; bahar se woh shut laga, lekin latch ka soft click nahi hua. Bina key-operated check ke woh sure nahi the ki locker properly secure hai. Khurana apne agle kaam ki taraf badh gaye.

Hospital ke is chhote se corridor mein ab tak teen alag knowledge states thi: Ritesh ko duplicate-key entry ka pata tha, Kunal apni unauthorized inspection jaanta tha, aur Khurana ne ek disturbed locker ko report kiye bina band kiya tha.

## M12 — Unease at the Locker

13:10–13:15 PM · Staff locker area · Episode 02 Meenakshi POV end · New draft for review

13:10 PM. Meenakshi nursing station se apna pending paperwork complete karke staff changing area mein aayi. Subah se lagataar kaam karne ke baad use kuch minute ki shaanti chahiye thi.

Usne apni coat ki pocket se locker key nikali. Door ke paas pahunchkar woh ek pal ruki.

Meenakshi locker ke seedha saamne ruki, key haath mein pakde. Door pehle jaisa firm nahi laga; latch thoda loose feel hua. Usne door khola toh gray cardigan ka fold alag direction mein tha, bag ka angle badla hua tha, aur pouch shelf ke left edge par sarki hui thi. Uski eyebrows thodi si upar uth gayi, lips press hue, aur usne pehle haath badhaya phir beech mein rok liya.

09:05 par usne lock carefully check kiya tha. Ab subah ka woh moment use bilkul saaf yaad aaya.

“Maine toh properly check kiya tha…”

Uski nazar ek-ek cheez par dobaara gayi; yeh sirf hastily folded cardigan ki baat nahi lag rahi thi.

Subah usne sab cheezein ek order mein rakhi thi—yeh uski aadat thi. Hospital mein itne saal kaam karne ke baad woh apna locker aur personal supplies carelessly chhodti nahi thi.

Meenakshi (halke se): “Yeh fold maine toh nahi kiya.”

Usne bag check kiya. Ek nazar mein koi obvious cheez missing nahi dikhi, lekin usse yeh bhi clear nahi tha ki kya-kya move hua tha. Woh kisi par bina evidence accusation nahi lagana chahti thi.

Ek pal woh andar ki cheezon ko dekhti rahi, phir phone nikaal kar current condition ki photo le li. Key aur latch ko dekhkar use laga shayad subah jaldi mein kuch bhool gayi ho; doosri taraf uske dimaag mein apni hi woh deliberate final check ghoom raha tha.

“Nahi. Main ise lock karke hi gayi thi.”

Uske chehre ki hansi dheere-dheere khatam ho gayi. Ab woh gusse se zyada alert thi. Kisne locker khola? Kab? Aur agar kisi ne khola, toh kyun?

13:15 PM. Meenakshi ne door ko dheere se band kar diya, key apne paas rakhi aur ek baar corridor ko dekha. Abhi uske paas kisi ka naam nahi tha, na koi confirmed explanation.

Meenakshi ka Episode 02 POV yahin rukta hai. Woh Harish–Sunil ke morning incident se bhi abhi unaware hai. Koi evening, ghar-waapsi ya next-day scene yahan add nahi hota.
