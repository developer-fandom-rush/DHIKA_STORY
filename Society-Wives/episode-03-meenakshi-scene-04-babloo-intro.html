Scene 04 · B01

An Unexpected Meeting

Society ka gate paar karte hi Meenakshi ne apni chaal thodi dheemi kar li. Paanch minute ki bike ride khatam ho chuki thi, lekin uske mann mein Uday aur Utkarsh ke saath hui shaam ki baatein abhi bhi chal rahi thi.

Usne apne plum kurte ko halka sa theek kiya. Cream palazzo ki silvatein seedhi ki aur loose baalon ko ek taraf kiya.

Uday ne bike rok kar uski taraf dekha.

Uday: “Ma’am, aaj badminton ka result toh pending reh gaya.”

Meenakshi: “Result? Tum dono ko abhi bhi lagta hai main haar gayi?”

Utkarsh: “Haar nahi gayi. Bas abhi training baaki hai.”

Meenakshi ne Utkarsh ko dekha. Uska wahi shaant expression tha jo court par Uday ki har teasing ke baad hota tha.

Meenakshi: “Achha ji. Agli baar dekh lenge.”

Uday hansa.

Uday: “Good night, ma’am. Aur haan, car ki tension mat lena.”

Meenakshi: “Tum dono bhi ghar pahunchkar message kar dena. Good night.”

Utkarsh ne muskurakar sir hilaya. Bike aage badh gayi.

Meenakshi Tower C ke entrance ki taraf mudi.

Entrance ke ek taraf Babloo pharmacy ke parcels arrange kar raha tha. Usne bike rukne ki awaaz suni thi aur nazar uthakar dekha bhi tha.

Uski nazar pehle jaate hue twins par gayi, phir Meenakshi par aakar ruk gayi.

Scene 04 · B02

The First Different Look

Babloo ne ek parcel rakhte-rakhte apna haath rok liya.

Meenakshi ko woh pehle se jaanta tha. Pharmacy pickups, deliveries aur medical supplies ke silsile mein unki baat hoti rehti thi. Babloo instructions dhyaan se follow karta tha, aur Meenakshi uski reliability ko appreciate karti thi.

Lekin aaj woh kisi kaam ke silsile mein saamne nahi aayi thi.

Plum kurta, cream palazzo aur khule baal. Din bhar ki busy routine ke baad bhi uske chehre par ek relaxed, cheerful expression tha.

Babloo ne use pehchaan liya, lekin aaj uski nazar routine greeting se ek pal zyada thehri.

Meenakshi ne entrance ke paas pahunchte hi uski taraf dekha.

Babloo seedha khada ho gaya.

Babloo: “Good evening, Doctor Madam.”

Meenakshi: “Good evening, Babloo.”

Woh do kadam aage badhi, phir ruk gayi.

Babloo ab bhi use dekh raha tha. Usne nazar milte hi halka sa smile kiya, lekin turant kuch nahi bola.

Meenakshi ki eyebrows thodi uthi.

Meenakshi (andar se): Yeh aaj itna chup kyun hai? Hamesha toh kaam ki poori report taiyaar rehti hai.

Uske chehre par ek chhoti si teasing smile aa gayi.

Scene 04 · B03

The Unexpected Compliment

Meenakshi: “Kya hua, Babloo? Aaj pehchaan nahi paaye?”

Babloo ek pal ko hansa. Phir usne seedha uski aankhon mein dekha.

Babloo: “Pehchaan toh gaya, Doctor Madam. Bas aaj aap kuch zyada hi khoobsurat lag rahi hain.”

Meenakshi ki smile ek second ke liye ruk gayi.

Compliment ki use umeed nahi thi — khaaskar Babloo se, jo aam taur par kaam ki baat karke turant apne agle pickup ki taraf badh jaata tha.

Meenakshi: “Achha? Toh ab tum bhi compliments dene lage?”

Babloo: “Sach bolne mein koi problem toh nahi hai, Madam?”

Meenakshi hansi.

Meenakshi: “Problem nahi hai. Bas tumhe aaj pehli baar aise bolte suna.”

Babloo ne apne haath mein rakha parcel theek kiya.

Babloo: “Pehle kabhi kehne ka mauka hi nahi mila.”

Meenakshi ne use dhyaan se dekha.

Meenakshi (andar se): Babloo? Itna confident? Yeh toh naya hai.

Woh jaanti thi ki Babloo apne kaam mein reliable hai. Lekin yeh pehli baar tha jab usne Babloo ki baat mein professional politeness se alag ek personal confidence notice kiya.

Meenakshi: “Waise Babloo, aaj kaam kam hai kya? Itni fursat mil rahi hai?”

Babloo: “Kaam toh hai, Madam. Bas aap saamne aa gayi toh do minute baat kar li.”

Meenakshi ne muskura kar sir hilaya.

Meenakshi: “Tum bhi na…”

Babloo ne uski smile dekhi. Woh khush tha ki uski baat ko Meenakshi ne awkward nahi banaya.

Scene 04 · B04

A Different Kind of Concern

Kuch pal baad Babloo ki nazar entrance ki taraf gayi.

Babloo: “Madam, ek baat poochun?”

Meenakshi: “Haan, bolo.”

Babloo: “Aapki car nahi dikhi. Abhi Uday bhaiya aur Utkarsh bhaiya ke saath aayi thi na?”

Meenakshi ke chehre ki smile thodi badli.

Meenakshi: “Haan yaar. Badminton court ke paas car start hi nahi hui.”

Babloo: “Achaanak?”

Meenakshi: “Haan. Do baar try kiya. Engine awaaz kar raha tha, lekin start nahi hua.”

Babloo ne dhyaan se suna.

Babloo: “Achha. Car wahin chhod di?”

Meenakshi: “Haan. Aur kya karti? Society paas thi, toh un dono ne drop kar diya.”

Babloo kuch sochne laga.

Babloo: “Kal aap bolo toh main chal kar dekh leta hoon. Mechanic ki zarurat hogi toh woh bhi arrange kar lenge.”

Meenakshi ne use dekha.

Abhi kuch der pehle Babloo uski tareef kar raha tha. Ab uski awaaz bilkul practical thi.

Meenakshi (andar se): Pehle compliment, ab bina kahe help bhi offer kar raha hai.

Meenakshi: “Tum dekh loge?”

Babloo: “Haan, Madam. Main check karwa dunga. Agar wahin repair nahi hui toh mechanic se recovery bhi pooch lenge.”

Meenakshi: “Achha hai. Kal dekhte hain phir.”

Babloo: “Aap tension mat lena.”

Meenakshi: “Thank you, Babloo. Tum waise bhi hamesha kaam time se kar dete ho.”

Babloo ke chehre par is baar pehle se alag smile thi.

Babloo: “Aap bharosa karti hain, Madam. Isliye dhyaan rakhta hoon.”

Meenakshi ki nazar uske chehre par ek pal ruki.

Usne notice kiya ki Babloo ka compliment aur uski help ki offer do alag baatein thi — lekin dono mein ek hi sincerity thi.

Scene 04 · B05

The Closing Hook

Lift lobby mein aakar Meenakshi ruk gayi.

Babloo ne parcels sambhale aur entrance ke paas hi khada raha.

Meenakshi: “Achha Babloo, kal car wala dekh lenge. Main ab chalti hoon.”

Babloo: “Ji, Doctor Madam. Good night.”

Meenakshi lift button dabane se pehle ek baar uski taraf mudi.

Meenakshi: “Aur haan…”

Babloo ne dekha.

Meenakshi: “Aaj bahut confidence aa gaya hai tum mein.”

Babloo halka sa sharmaaya, phir muskuraya.

Babloo: “Aapne hi toh poochha tha, Madam.”

Meenakshi hans padi.

Meenakshi: “Achha baba. Good night.”

Babloo: “Good night, Doctor Madam.”

Lift ka darwaaza khula.

Meenakshi andar chali gayi.

Darwaaza band hone se pehle uski nazar ek aakhri baar entrance ki taraf gayi. Babloo apne parcels ke paas wapas jaa chuka tha.

Meenakshi (andar se): Babloo toh hamesha se achha ladka hai… lekin aaj kuch alag hi confidence tha usmein.

Uske chehre par halki si smile reh gayi.

Udhar Babloo ne agla parcel uthaya.

Babloo (andar se): Doctor Madam ko meri baat buri nahi lagi. Aur kal car ke liye unki help bhi karni hai.

Woh wapas apne kaam mein lag gaya.

Lift upar badhne lagi.

Meenakshi ke liye ek lambi shaam khatam ho rahi thi. Babloo ke saath ek nayi personal understanding ki bas shuruaat hui thi.

Original author-approved plot outline · B01–B05 (reference only)

Babloo (23) society mein pharmacy pickups aur deliveries sambhalta hai. Meenakshi use pehle se jaanti hai aur uski reliable service par bharosa karti hai. Woh delivery instructions carefully follow karta hai. Abhi tak unka relationship familiar doctor–helper ka hai; koi romantic encounter establish nahi hua.

B01 · Unexpected meeting

At around 6:45 PM the twins have just dropped Meenakshi after badminton. Babloo is arranging pharmacy parcels near Tower C. He catches the boys’ playful goodbye but is not involved in it.

B02 · First different look

Babloo notices Meenakshi's loose hair, plum kurta’s hourglass silhouette, cheerful expression and softer-than-usual smile. His appreciative gaze holds for a moment and Meenakshi notices.

B03 · Flirtation

Meenakshi starts with a teasing question. Babloo responds with an unexpectedly confident compliment; she smiles and continues the playful exchange.

B04 · Concern beyond appearance

Babloo notices that Meenakshi's own car has not returned. She explains the car stopped working near the badminton court. He genuinely offers to help check or recover it tomorrow.

B05 · Closing hook

Meenakshi agrees to Babloo's help with her car the following day. They part by the lift. She recalls his unusual confidence as she walks away, and Babloo remembers her positive response to his compliment.
