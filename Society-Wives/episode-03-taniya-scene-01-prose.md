**TANIYA · EPISODE 03 · SCENE 01 — THE WEDDING GIRL**
**1 January 2026 · 10:50–11:05 AM · CA Firm, Gurgaon · Audit Desk**

10:50 AM.

December-close ka New Year shift 10:30 se chal raha tha. Taniya ne apna laptop bag chair ke paas rakha hua tha. Sky-blue formal shirt aur charcoal trousers mein woh bank-reconciliation sheet par red-marked entries cross-check kar rahi thi. Neha aur Andrea uski desk ke ek taraf, Raghav aur Chirag doosri taraf familiar team rhythm mein kaam kar rahe the.

**Neha:** "Happy New Year, future Mrs. Aditya! Aaj bhi tumne spreadsheet khol rakhi hai?"

**Taniya:** "Happy New Year. Client ka December calendar shaadi ki dates dekh ke band nahi hota, Neha."

**Chirag:** "Sahi kaha. Hamara countdown twelve baje nahi, trial balance match hone pe khatam hoga."

**Raghav:** "Aur tumhara countdown hoga: twelve hours of overtime?"

**Andrea:** "Raghav, please. New Year par kam se kam ek original joke toh laao."

Raghav ne haath uthakar defeat accept ki. Yeh chaaron mahino se ek hi team mein kaam kar rahe the; aise exchanges mein kisi naye introduction ki zaroorat nahi padti thi.

Neha ne screen par December 31 ki ek unmatched journal entry point ki.

**Neha:** "Yeh dekho. Payment bank mein thirty-first ko gaya, ledger mein January first show ho raha hai."

**Taniya:** "Date mat guess karo. Bank reference aur posting timestamp alag columns mein verify karo. Phir decide karenge exception hai ya cut-off entry."

**Chirag:** "Maine branch-wise reconciliation nikaali hai. Raghav, tum original reference check kar lo."

**Raghav:** "Ji, sir. Aur jab main check kar lunga toh credit mere naam?"

**Andrea:** "Credit ke liye bhi evidence chahiye. Tumhari smiles audit evidence nahi hoti."

Taniya hansi aur apni chair se uthkar printer ke paas gayi. Ek revised schedule uthate hue usne pages ko seedha kiya.

11:03 AM.

Punit audit register aur ek fresh stationery pad lekar workstations ke paas aaya. Woh team ko pehle se jaanta tha; Taniya ki desk par files chhodte hue routine update diya.

**Punit:** "Ma'am, last quarter ka binder records shelf par mil gaya. Register mein entry kar di hai."

**Taniya:** "Thanks, Punit. Usse wahin rakho. Noon tak mujhe journal-support index bhi chahiye."

Neha ne halka sa salute kiya. Punit ne bas sir hilaaya aur apne kaam par laut gaya. Uske saath lambi personal conversation abhi shuru nahi hui.

11:05 AM.

Chirag ne reconciliation worksheet save ki. Raghav filing cabinet ki taraf badha, Taniya original bank reference verify karne ke liye uske saath chali. Neha aur Andrea apne screens par workpapers open rakhkar aapas mein exception list discuss karne lagin.

**Taniya:** "Raghav, drawer zara dhyaan se kholna. Last month se atak raha hai."

**Raghav:** "Haan. Pehle reference, phir heroics."

Taniya ne ek eyebrow uthayi. "Heroics" ka matlab abhi sirf files tak tha.
