Meenakshi · M02 and M03

1 January 2026 · 07:45–08:15 AM · Gulmohar Heights, Gurgaon

Approved-for-publication draft. M01-F02 bathing / getting-ready transition appears at the end of M01. This page continues with twins and the society gate. No afternoon or evening scenes are added.

M02 — Two Contrasting Wellness Twins

07:45–08:10 AM · Society wellness area

07:45 AM. Tower C se nikalte hi Meenakshi ne society ke wellness corner ki taraf kadam badhaye. Agle health camp ke volunteer register ka quick check baaki tha. Uday aur Utkarsh wahin supplies ki list dekh rahe the.

Dono bhaiyon ko woh kareeb ek saal se jaanti thi. Medical camps mein unhone chairs, registration desk aur basic supplies sambhalne mein kaafi madad ki thi. Uday hamesha zyada khulkar bolta; Utkarsh kam, lekin sochkar. Shuru mein dono ke jokes use kuch zyada lagte the. Ab woh unki reliability aur unke teasing andaaz, dono ko achhi tarah pehchaan gayi thi.

Uday: Good morning, Doctor sahiba! Aaj white coat ke bina?

Meenakshi: White coat hospital mein pehnungi. Yahan volunteer register lene aayi hoon. Tum dono ne ready kiya?

Utkarsh: List ready hai. Lekin Uday ko aaj morning fashion ki bhi ek report deni thi.

Meenakshi: Achha? Woh kab se dress consultant ban gaya?

Uday: Consultant nahi. Bas itna keh raha hoon ki plum kurta aur grey cardigan ka combination dangerous hai. Cardigan cover kar raha hai, lekin kurte ki fitting saara secret nahi chhupa rahi.

Meenakshi ne eyebrow uthayi. Woh samajh gayi ki Uday kis taraf ishara kar raha hai. Ek saal pehle shayad isi line par use chup kara deti; aaj usne register ke bajay uski taraf dekhkar muskurana chuna.

Meenakshi: Toh ab tum dono mere kapdon ki fitting bhi evaluate karoge?

Utkarsh: Main toh ise rok raha tha. Aapne khud pooch liya, ab detailed report rokna mushkil hai.

Meenakshi: Aur tumhari report? Itne seedhe mat bano.

Utkarsh: Meri report simple hai: aaj aapne hume notice karne ka mauka diya, aur phir khud notice kar liya ki hum notice kar rahe hain.

Meenakshi: Tum chup rehkar zyada dangerous ho, Utkarsh.

Uday: Ek saal pehle toh aap hamari aisi baaton par seedha lecture deti thi.

Meenakshi: Haan, kyunki tab tum dono ko tameez nahi thi. Aur camp ki inventory mein bhi har baar ek na ek column missing hota tha.

Utkarsh: Ab inventory complete hai. Tameez ka status?

Meenakshi: Tameez thodi aa gayi hai. Lekin tumhari nazron ka treatment abhi baaki hai.

Uday: Toh treatment kab start hoga?

Meenakshi: Jab tum dono decide kar loge ki tumhe doctor ki advice chahiye ya doctor ka attention. Dono alag cheezein hain.

Utkarsh: Phir toh Uday ko second opinion chahiye hoga.

Uday: Aur tum? Tum itni der se bilkul healthy behave kar rahe ho?

Meenakshi: Dono ko diagnosis mil chuka hai: excessive talking. Ab dekho supplies list.

Teenon hans diye. Baatein dobara gloves, registration sheets aur next camp ke practical kaam par aa gayin. Utkarsh ne register handover kiya aur Meenakshi ne missing items ke aage chhoti si tick lagayi. Uday ne bina kisi aur comment ke list verify karne mein madad ki.

08:09. Gate ki taraf chalte hue Meenakshi ko Utkarsh ki ek line yaad reh gayi: “Aaj aapne hume notice karne ka mauka diya.” Woh khud se muskurayi. Aaj usne unki attention se bachne ki koshish nahi ki thi. Kya usse ab unke agle clever jawab ka intezaar rehne laga tha? Is khayal ko usne turant koi naam nahi diya.

M03 — A Brief Stop at the Society Gate

08:10–08:15 AM · Gulmohar Heights gate

08:10 AM. Gate par Gopal ne Meenakshi ki gaadi dekhi aur routine delivery register ke saath aage aaya.

Gopal: Good morning, Doctor madam. Pharmacy delivery ka confirmation mil gaya. Receipt mein family member ka naam bhi hai.

Meenakshi: Good. Jo naam form par hai, packet sirf unhi ko dena. Kisi aur ke haath mat bhejna.

Gopal: Ji madam. Aaj bhi hospital duty?

Meenakshi: Haan. Aur tumne New Year par bhi duty le li?

Gopal: Madam, society ke log chhutti le sakte hain. Gate ko toh chhutti nahi milti.

Meenakshi: Toh agle health camp mein tumhara bhi check-up due hai. Bahane nahi chalenge.

Gopal: Aap khud check karengi toh zaroor aaunga.

Meenakshi: Health camp hai, special appointment counter nahi!

Gopal muskuraya aur barrier hata diya. Meenakshi ne haath hilaaya aur gaadi aage badha di.

Gopal ki private nazar mein Meenakshi hamesha se khoobsurat thi. Maa banne ke baad uski appearance mein aaye badlav bhi usse nazar aate the. Aaj ka plum kurta uske dimaag mein reh gaya. “Madam ko dekh kar din achha lagta hai. Hamari kismat mein toh bas good morning hi hai.” Usne yeh soch khud tak rakhi aur wapas register mein entry karne laga.

08:15 AM. Meenakshi hospital commute par thi. Uday–Utkarsh ki spoken flirting, Gopal ki unspoken admiration, aur Mohan ki alag private secrecy — teen alag knowledge states the. Meenakshi ko Mohan aur Gopal ke man ki baat ka pata nahi tha.
