## SCENE 01 — The Water-Filter Accident (18:25–18:45)

### Established event sequence (A01–A05)
- **A01, 18:25–18:28:** At the Episode 03 doorbell cliff, Ankita opens the door. Rinku (23) is a *previously familiar* building helper, not a stranger. He brings a small tool pouch and washer and follows Ankita to the kitchen. Her homewear is an ordinary cotton kurti and relaxed trousers, without a dupatta. Color/neckline/fit are not locked.
- **A02, 18:28–18:32:** Rinku crouches by the sink cabinet to check the coupler. Ankita is near the reachable shutoff valve with a dry towel. The coupler has a hairline crack; he cautions that pressure may remain.
- **A03, 18:32–18:34:** The loosened cracked coupler abruptly releases a concentrated water burst. It wets Ankita’s forearm and outer kurti and makes part of the tile slippery. She catches Rinku’s **clothed upper arm for balance**, shoulders close for **2–3 seconds**, then releases once steady. Neither planned this contact.
- **A04, 18:34–18:39:** Water makes the **existing inner clothing outline** briefly discernible through the wet outer fabric; **no bare breast or nudity**. Rinku looks for an instant, averts his eyes, and returns to work. Ankita notices his unusual silence but does not know its cause.
- **A05, 18:39–18:45:** He secures a **temporary** repair, asks for a matching connector before permanent pressure testing, and leaves. Ankita later changes into dry clothing and privately wonders whether his silence was about the repair or something else.

**PURPLE / already-written tension:** Changed fabric outline, momentary gaze and averted attention, Ankita’s unanswered internal question. The touch itself was an involuntary balance reaction, not proof of attraction.

**BLUE / precise physical facts:** Burst direction from coupler; kurti/forearm wetted; slick tile; clothed upper arm held for 2–3 seconds; temporary repair and polite exit.

**GREY / what did not happen:** No breast exposure, lingerie removal, kiss, deliberate second touch, sexual act, confession, Rinku re-entry, third-party witness, or parcel knowledge.

**AMBER V01 / Grok or author visual review:** Clarify non-explicit spatial geometry of sink cabinet, valve, footing, jet direction, towel and reach; how the wet outer layer changed, without inventing an unconfirmed outfit design.
