**10:50 AM**

December-close ka New Year shift 10:30 se chal raha tha. Taniya ne apna laptop bag chair ke paas rakha hua tha. Sky-blue formal shirt aur charcoal trousers mein woh bank-reconciliation sheet par red-marked entries cross-check kar rahi thi. Shirt ka top button open tha, collar thoda loose, aur trousers uski waist pe snug the.

Neha aur Andrea uski desk ke ek taraf, Raghav aur Chirag doosri taraf familiar team rhythm mein kaam kar rahe the.

**Neha:** “Happy New Year, future Mrs. Aditya! Aaj bhi tumne spreadsheet khol rakhi hai?”

**Taniya:** “Happy New Year. Client ka December calendar shaadi ki dates dekh ke band nahi hota, Neha.”

**Chirag:** “Sahi kaha. Hamara countdown twelve baje nahi, trial balance match hone pe khatam hoga.”

**Raghav:** “Aur tumhara countdown hoga: twelve hours of overtime?”

**Andrea:** “Raghav, please. New Year par kam se kam ek original joke toh laao.”

Raghav ne haath utha kar defeat accept ki. Yeh chaaron mahinon se ek hi team mein kaam kar rahe the.

Neha ne screen par December 31 ki ek unmatched journal entry point ki.

**Neha:** “Yeh dekho. Payment bank mein thirty-first ko gaya, ledger mein January first show ho raha hai.”

**Taniya:** “Date mat guess karo. Bank reference aur posting timestamp alag columns mein verify karo. Phir decide karenge exception hai ya cut-off entry.”

**Chirag:** “Maine branch-wise reconciliation nikaali hai. Raghav, tum original reference check kar lo.”

**Raghav:** “Ji, sir. Aur jab main check kar lunga toh credit mere naam?”

**Andrea:** “Credit ke liye bhi evidence chahiye. Tumhari smiles audit evidence nahi hoti.”

Taniya hansi aur apni chair se uthkar printer ke paas gayi. Ek revised schedule uthate hue usne pages seedhe kiye.

**Neha:** “Honeymoon leave bhi audit approval ke baad milegi kya?”

**Taniya:** “Tum pehle apni leave balance check kar lo. Meri leave ka budget already calculate ho chuka hai.”

**Andrea:** “Aditya ko bata dena, inki checking bahut strict hai.”

**Raghav:** “Aditya ko warning do ya training schedule? Taniya se koi half-filled form pass nahi hota.”

**Taniya:** “Pehle apni files close karo, phir meri shaadi audit karna.”

**Chirag:** “Record mein note ho gaya: bride-to-be refuses unofficial audit.”

Neha ne phone ki taraf haath badhaya, jaise imaginary minutes of meeting record karne wali ho.

**Neha:** “Agenda item two: shaadi ke baad Taniya madam weekend calls bhi reject karengi?”

**Taniya:** “Agar client ne tumhe Saturday ko call kiya, toh tum mera naam mat lena.”

**Andrea:** “Dekha? Itni confidently mana karti hai. Aditya ko negotiation practice karni padegi.”

**Taniya:** “Usse meri negotiation skills ka pata hai. Tumhe apna pending voucher ka pata hai?”

Andrea ne screen ki taraf jhaank kar nakli seriousness dikha di.

**10:57 AM**

Raghav ne Taniya ke haath mein revised schedule dekha. Uski quick replies familiar thin; phir bhi uski smile ek pal ko bina joke ke reh gayi.

Usne nazar wapas report par kar li. Yeh baat usne kisi se share nahi ki — aur Taniya ne us pal ko notice bhi nahi kiya.

**Grok visual insertion G02**

Raghav ki nazar Taniya ke sky-blue shirt aur charcoal trousers pe ek second zyada ruki. Uski fitted waist aur confident stance uske liye clearly attractive thi, lekin usne turant aankhein report pe laa di. Uske chehre pe koi open reaction nahi tha — sirf andar se ek quiet, unspoken pull. Taniya is moment se completely unaware thi.

**Chirag:** “Raghav? Original bank ref mil gaya ya tum ab bhi celebration mode mein ho?”

**Raghav:** “Mil jayega. Filing drawer mein December ka physical folder hona chahiye.”

**Taniya:** “Good. Digital trail se amount match hai; physical reference missing hai. Pehle woh nikaalte hain.”

**Neha:** “Main summary clean kar deti hoon. Andrea, tum exceptions list filter kar do.”

**Andrea:** “Done. Lekin lunch pe wedding stories ka second round booked hai.”

**Taniya:** “Client approval ke baad.”

**Andrea:** “Tumhari dictionary mein romance bhi approval-dependent hai kya?”

Taniya ne file band ki aur Andrea ki taraf ek pointed smile diya.

**Taniya:** “Tum logon ke imagination par main sign nahi karti. Workpapers par karti hoon.”

**Chirag:** “Finally, ek policy jo clearly written hai.”

**11:03 AM**

Punit audit register aur ek fresh stationery pad lekar workstations ke paas aaya. Usne Taniya ki desk par files chhode aur routine update diya.

**Punit:** “Ma'am, last quarter ka binder records shelf par mil gaya. Register mein entry kar di hai.”

**Taniya:** “Thanks, Punit. Usse wahin rakho. Noon tak mujhe journal-support index bhi chahiye.”

**Punit:** “Noted, ma'am.”

Neha ne halka-sa salute kiya. Punit ne sir hilaya aur apne kaam par laut gaya.

**11:05 AM**

Chirag ne reconciliation worksheet save ki. Raghav filing cabinet ki taraf badha, Taniya original bank reference verify karne ke liye uske saath chali.

**Taniya:** “Raghav, drawer zara dhyaan se kholna. Last month se atak raha hai.”

**Raghav:** “Haan. Pehle reference, phir heroics.”

Taniya ne ek eyebrow uthayi. “Heroics” ka matlab abhi sirf files tak tha.

**Scene 01 ends.**
