## Authoritative save-state definitions
- **Approved plot**: Story beat/visual/timing is approved, but it does not guarantee complete scene prose.
- **Working prose**: Draft text published for review, not frozen by the author.
- **Verbatim handoff**: Raw author instructions exist; Grok's complete final scene has **not** been written.
- **Image reference**: May show an incorrect face, dress, anatomy or chronology. Rejected images are stored ONLY for comparison.

## Conflicts and necessary caution
1. Do not call Ankita–Rohit Scene03 'complete prose'. It is verbatim directions; the separate GPT draft is not final.
2. Do not claim the Rinku story is fully expanded just because 13 scene cards are present.
3. Do not call the Chintu indigo generated face the canonical Shreya identity; Keshav red outfit approval does not approve a facial reference.
4. Shreya Tue/Wed January6–7 events are **candidate clocks**, not established residence/shift/childcare facts.
5. A past request had removed images from some story UI surfaces; this new archive keeps references visible only by link/index, without silently restoring all images into the Reader.
6. Saved local images and the Library ZIP do not become public web image URLs. Upload to GitHub or another public asset host requires an explicit separate publication workflow.

**This audit deliberately lists missing work instead of marking it complete.**
