T01 — A Familiar Court, a Different Evening

Meenakshi ne car park ki aur badminton court ki taraf badhi. Plum kurta abhi bhi uspe tha, cream palazzo neeche, grey cardigan haath mein. Baal thode loose ho gaye the farmhouse ke baad. Uski chaal normal thi, lekin mann abhi bhi pehle wali shaam ke kuch moments pe atka hua tha.

Court pe Uday aur Utkarsh rally khatam kar rahe the. Dono sportswear mein the — shorts aur t-shirts, thoda pasina. Shuttle last point pe gaya. Uday ne Meenakshi ko pehle dekha.

Uday: “Arre Meenakshi ma’am! Aap yahan? Subah ke baad phir se?”

Utkarsh ne racket neeche kiya aur uski taraf dekha. Uski nazar thodi der ruki — kurta ka fit, loose baal, cardigan haath mein.

Utkarsh: “Shaam ko alag lag rahi hain. Subah se.”

Meenakshi unke paas pahunchi. Subah wala playful tone usse yaad aa gaya.

Meenakshi: “Achha, tum dono yahan practice karte ho? Khatam nahi hui abhi?”

Uday: “Ho gayi almost. Aap aa gayi toh extra energy aa gayi.”

Meenakshi hasi. Farmhouse ki tension thodi kam hui. Woh unke saath present hone lagi.

Meenakshi: “Energy toh tum dono mein pehle se zyada hai. Subah bhi yahi lag raha tha.”

Utkarsh ne shuttle uthaya. Uski nazar abhi bhi uspe thi, lekin Uday jaisi open nahi.

Utkarsh: “Aap thaki hui lag rahi hain. Lamba din tha kya?”

Meenakshi ne cardigan side pe rakha.

Meenakshi: “Thoda. Tum log khel rahe ho, main dekh lun?”

Dono ne ek doosre ko dekha. Uday hasa. Utkarsh ne racket uski taraf thoda sa uthaya, jaise offer kar raha ho. Meenakshi court ke kinare khadi ho gayi. Shaam ki hawa mein uska kurta halka sa hil raha tha.

T02 — The Morning Fashion Report Returns

Uday ne racket side pe rakha aur Meenakshi ki taraf muskuraya.

Uday: “Ma’am, subah wala fashion report continue karein? Subah cardigan ke saath plum kurta tha. Ab cardigan haath mein hai. Fashion report update karni padegi.”

Meenakshi ne uski taraf dekha.

Meenakshi: “Tum abhi bhi wahi report de rahe ho? Badminton ke baad bhi?”

Uday: “Haan. Professional habit hai.”

Utkarsh ne towel se face pochha. Uski nazar Meenakshi ke kurta ke fit pe gayi, phir upar.

Utkarsh: “Report mein yeh add karo. Subah cardigan tha, toh fitting utni clear nahi thi. Ab difference notice ho raha hai.”

Uday ruk gaya. Meenakshi ne Utkarsh ki taraf dekha. Yeh Uday jaisa open nahi tha.

Meenakshi: “Utkarsh, tum toh subah itna bolte nahi the.”

Utkarsh: “Subah aap jaldi mein thi. Ab time hai.”

Meenakshi hasi. Usne dono ko dekha — Uday openly grinning, Utkarsh calm lekin direct. Dono ke shorts aur t-shirts pasine se chipke hue the, arms aur shoulders clearly dikh rahe the.

Meenakshi: “Tum dono ki bhi report lun? Badminton ke baad tumhara athletic look kaafi strong lag raha hai. Especially arms.”

Uday: “Ma’am, aap bhi notice kar rahi hain?”

Meenakshi: “Haan. Sirf tum log hi dekhte ho kya?”

Utkarsh ne halka sa smile diya.

Utkarsh: “Aap subah se zyada relaxed lag rahi hain. Isliye zyada bol rahe hain.”

Meenakshi: “Shayad. Tum dono ke saath maza aata hai. Subah bhi aaya tha.”

Uday ne Utkarsh ki taraf dekha.

Uday: “Yeh toh aaj alag mode mein hai.”

Meenakshi ne dono ke beech ki nazar pakdi aur has di. Woh unke saath comfortable ho rahi thi. Farmhouse ki baatein thodi door ho gayi thin. Court pe ab teeno ki conversation chal rahi thi, aur Meenakshi khud usme participate kar rahi thi.

T03 — The Badminton Challenge

Meenakshi ne court ki taraf dekha.

Meenakshi: “Tum log roz shaam ko yahan khelte ho?”

Uday: “Haan. Almost roz. Utkarsh ko lagta hai main weak hoon, isliye extra practice.”

Utkarsh: “Tum khud kehte ho.”

Meenakshi hasi.

Meenakshi: “Mujhe bhi try karna hai. Racket doge?”

Dono ruke. Uday ne turant racket uthaya.

Uday: “Aap kurta-palazzo mein khelogi?”

Meenakshi: “Haan. Problem hai?”

Utkarsh: “Nahi. Bas dekhte hain kitna confidence hai.”

Uday ne apna racket uske haath mein diya. Meenakshi ne pakda. Thoda heavy laga. Uday ne shuttle upar phenka. Meenakshi ne swing kiya. Shuttle side mein gaya.

Uday: “Arre! Power toh hai.”

Meenakshi phir se try ki. Is baar shuttle net ke upar se gaya lekin out.

Meenakshi: “Ek aur.”

Utkarsh ne shuttle uthaya aur uske saamne fenka. Meenakshi ne stance liya, kurta thoda tight hua waist pe. Swing kiya. Shuttle seedha court mein gaya.

Utkarsh: “Yeh better tha.”

Meenakshi hasi, saans thodi tez. Uday ne taali bajayi.

Uday: “Ma’am, aap toh seekh jayengi jaldi.”

Meenakshi: “Dekhte hain. Tum log sikhao sahi se.”

Woh racket ghumati rahi. Dono usse dekh rahe the, lekin Meenakshi khud actively try kar rahi thi. Court pe ab teeno the.

T04 — Uday Teaches Her Stance

Uday Meenakshi ke paas aaya.

Uday: “Ma’am, stance galat hai. Main dikhata hoon.”

Woh uske peeche aa gaya. Dono haath uske shoulders pe rakhe aur use side-on position mein ghuma diya. Meenakshi ne allow kiya.

Uday: “Shoulders aise. Ek aage, ek peeche. Waist tight rakho.”

Usne apna haath uski waist pe rakha aur thoda adjust kiya. Kurta ke through usne pressure feel kiya. Meenakshi ki saans thodi change hui.

Meenakshi: “Uday, tum toh bahut serious ho gaye teaching mein.”

Uday: “Haan. Aap seekh rahi ho na.”

Woh aur close aaya. Uska chest uske back se halka sa touch hua. Racket wala haath usne Meenakshi ke haath pe rakha aur swing ka movement dikhaya. Meenakshi ne uske arms ke muscles feel kiye.

Meenakshi: “Tumhe yeh sikhana bahut pasand aa raha hai lagta hai.”

Uday: “Pasand aa raha hai. Aap allow kar rahi ho.”

Utkarsh side se dekh raha tha. Uday ki enthusiasm usse clearly dikh rahi thi.

Utkarsh: “Uday, zyada acting mat kar. Technique toh main better dikha sakta hoon.”

Meenakshi ne Utkarsh ki taraf dekha aur has di. Uday abhi bhi uske peeche khada tha, haath uski waist pe.

Meenakshi: “Achha? Toh dikhao.”

Uday ne haath hataya, lekin smile uske chehre pe thi. Utkarsh aage badha.

T05 — Utkarsh Surprises Everyone

Utkarsh aage aaya. Uday side mein hat gaya.

Utkarsh: “Uday ne sirf position dikhayi. Balance nahi.”

Woh Meenakshi ke bagal mein khada hua. Racket uske haath se liya aur khud stance dikhaya — calm, controlled.

Utkarsh: “Core tight rakho. Weight beech mein. Aise.”

Usne Meenakshi ka haath pakda aur apni taraf thoda khincha. Phir uski waist pe ek haath rakha, dheere se. Pressure Uday se kam tha, lekin zyada deliberate.

Utkarsh: “Yahan se move hota hai. Shoulder se nahi.”

Meenakshi ne uski taraf dekha. Utkarsh ki nazar seedha uski aankhon mein thi. Woh expect nahi kar rahi thi itna confident touch.

Meenakshi: “Tum toh Uday se zyada serious lag rahe ho.”

Utkarsh: “Serious nahi. Sahi kar raha hoon.”

Usne haath hataya aur racket Meenakshi ko lauta diya. Meenakshi ki saans thodi tez thi. Uday side se dekh raha tha, expression thoda tight.

Meenakshi (andar se): Yeh Uday jaisa nahi hai. Yeh jaan-bujh ke kar raha hai.

T06 — Twin Rivalry

Uday ne racket uthaya.

Uday: “Utkarsh, tumhe toh aaj bahut attention mil gaya. Subah toh itna bolte nahi the.”

Utkarsh: “Subah tum zyada bol rahe the. Aaj main.”

Meenakshi ne dono ko dekha aur has di.

Meenakshi: “Tum dono ki rivalry mujhe subah se dikh rahi hai. Ab toh clear ho gayi.”

Uday: “Ma’am, aap uski side le rahi hain?”

Meenakshi: “Nahi. Dono ki le rahi hoon. Tum openly sikhate ho. Yeh bina bole zyada kar leta hai.”

Utkarsh ne halka sa smile diya.

Utkarsh: “Aap notice kar leti hain.”

Uday: “Haan, bahut zyada.”

Meenakshi ne racket wapas Uday ko diya.

Meenakshi: “Bas. Aaj ke liye itna. Tum dono practice karo. Main chalti hoon.”

Dono ne usse jaate dekha. Uday ne Utkarsh ki taraf dekha.

Uday: “Aaj tum aage nikal gaye.”

Utkarsh: “Dekhte hain.”

Meenakshi court se bahar nikal gayi, cardigan haath mein. Uski chaal normal thi, lekin mann mein Utkarsh ka calm touch abhi bhi ghoom raha tha.
