## M06 — Between Rounds

09:05–10:15 AM · Hospital rounds · Draft for review

09:05 AM. Meenakshi ne ward ke nursing station par register sign kiya. Rupa night-shift handover lekar taiyaar khadi thi; Dr. Aditi ne pending investigation sheets ko room order mein lagaya. Avi aur Yash ko pata tha kaunsi files Meenakshi pehle dekhti hai—yeh koi nayi team nahi thi.

Rupa: “Bed twelve ki repeat report aa gayi. Medicines chart update hai; discharge pe abhi hold rakha hai.”

Meenakshi: “Good. Aditi, pehle symptoms aur vitals compare karenge. Avi, tum differential bataoge. Yash, investigation trend. Guess karne se better hai data dekhna.”

Agle aadhe ghante mein jokes ka waqt nahi tha. Meenakshi ne bedside questions poochhe, family ko treatment samjhaya aur trainee answers mein do galtiyan calmly correct ki. Ek patient ke saamne Avi ka confident guess galat nikla; Meenakshi ne use sabke saamne embarrass karne ke bajay baad mein corridor mein reasoning samjha di. Yash ne uske kehne se pehle follow-up chart nikaal diya.

09:34 AM. Do rooms ke beech chhota break mila. Patient corridor se alag staff-only alcove mein Meenakshi ne coat ka front button khola aur coffee ki ek sip li. Open coat ke dono panels halki si taraf hat gaye; neeche fitted plum kurti ne uski waist ka shape saaf kar diya. Avi ki nazar ek pal wahan tik gayi. Meenakshi ne turant pakad li—use pehle bhi kai baar aise pakda tha.

Meenakshi: “Avi, file par jo number hai woh padho. Mere waist par likha hua nahi milega.”

Avi: “Kisne kaha main number dhoondh raha tha? Aaj white coat ki fitting aapki kamar par kuch zyada hi noticeable hai.”

Yash ne chart par nazar rakhi, lekin uske honthon par smile aa gayi. Meenakshi ne cup neeche rakha, ek second ke liye coat ke open front ko adjust kiya, aur Avi ki taraf seedhi, challenging nazar daali. Usne apni posture badalne ki jaldi nahi ki. Pehle aisi line par woh Avi ko seedha lecture de deti; aaj uska jawab aane se pehle hi uski aankhon mein ek teasing warning thi.

Meenakshi: “Dheere bolo. Tumhari nazar ka audit hua na, findings report se zyada interesting niklengi.”

Avi: “Toh inspection aap khud karengi?”

Meenakshi: “Main inspection nahi karti. Main decide karti hoon kiski access revoke karni hai.”

Avi has pada. Meenakshi bhi muskuraayi—lekin usne turant file khol di. Khel ka yeh hissa sab samajhte the: staff-only waqt mein teasing, ward ke andar full professionalism.

09:48 AM. Agla round serious tha. Meenakshi ne ek patient's medication discrepancy notice ki. Aditi ko prescription cross-check karaya, Yash se last dose verify karayi aur Rupa ko revised handover diya. Avi ne bina drama ke pharmacy call ki. Kaam ke waqt sab ka focus ek hi jagah tha.

10:03 AM. Reports counter ke paas Naveen updated duty-sheet laaya. Paperwork clear karke woh chala gaya. Yash ne Meenakshi ke saamne ek imaging note rakha. Woh screen par ek faint detail dikhane laga. Screen ka angle galat tha; Meenakshi uske paas khadi ho gayi. Screen padhne ke liye woh Yash ke shoulder ke bilkul kareeb aa gayi. Yash ki nazar ek pal uske open coat ke front se screen par gayi, phir wapas Meenakshi ke chehre par; usne apni awaaz normal rakhi.

Yash: “Yahan dekhiye. Last reading se subtle difference hai.”

Meenakshi: “Screen thoda meri taraf karo. Tum itna door kyun khade ho? Main kaat nahi khaungi.”

Yash: “Aapka confidence dekhkar kabhi-kabhi risk assessment badalna padta hai.”

Meenakshi: “Tum bhi Avi ki tarah double meaning seekh gaye?”

Yash: “Avi sab bol deta hai. Main jo notice karta hoon, uska aadha bhi nahi bolta.”

Meenakshi ne screen se nazar hataakar use dekha. Yash ki awaaz usual se neeche thi. Uske kareeb khade hone ka ehsaas Meenakshi ne pehli baar apne dhyaan mein aate hue pakda. Usne khud ko ek inch peeche nahi kiya. Yash ne file pakadne ke liye haath badhaya toh uski ungliyaan Meenakshi ke sleeve se halki si takra gayin. Meenakshi ne apna haath turant nahi hataya; uski saans ek pal ko thami aur phir woh normal hui. Yash ne uska rukna notice kiya, lekin khud ek kadam peeche ho gaya—jaise poochhe bina kisi boundary ko cross nahi karna chahta tha.

Meenakshi: “Itni jaldi peeche? Abhi toh tum bade brave ban rahe the.”

Yash: “Brave hone aur bina poochhe paas rehne mein farq hai.”

Meenakshi: “Achha hai. Kam se kam tumhe farq pata hai.”

Kuch seconds ke liye dono ke beech woh familiar chhed-chhad se alag ek silence raha. Meenakshi ko Yash ka restrained andaaz achha laga—sirf uska compliment nahi, balki uska space dena bhi. Ek chhoti si, unwanted nahi balki surprising physical curiosity uske andar reh gayi.

Yash: “Waise, doctor, aap jab ekdum paas aakar screen padhti hain, meri attention do jagah baant jaati hai.”

Meenakshi: “Toh aankhon ka ilaaj karao. Aur report ki line miss ki toh aaj koi special consideration nahi.”

Yash: “Special consideration ka form kahan milta hai?”

Meenakshi: “Form toh mil jayega. Lekin ek condition hai—pehle tumhe clearly batana padega ki report padh rahe ho ya mujhe. Aur haan, permission wali line sabse upar hoti hai.”

Yash hansa. Yash: “Toh pehle honesty check, phir approval? Khatarnak department hai aapka.” Meenakshi ne bhauhen uthayi. Meenakshi: “Galat jagah application daaloge toh rejection bhi personal lagega.” Dono hans diye. Meenakshi ne report par final note lagaya. Yash ka haath ab screen se door tha, lekin uski woh ek line aur uska turant peeche hatna Meenakshi ko yaad reh gaya.

10:12 AM. Rupa ne rounds complete hone ki information di. Aditi ko follow-up task diya gaya; Avi aur Yash ne pending charts settle kiye. Naveen ne 10:20 ki senior consultation ka confirmation bhej diya.

10:15 AM. Meenakshi conference corridor ki taraf chal di. Subah society mein twins ka teasing aur ab hospital mein Avi–Yash ki familiarity uske dimaag mein ek pal ko mil gayi. Woh jaanti thi attention aur invitation alag cheezein hain. Aaj pehli baar use bhi kisi ki physical closeness ko lekar apni curiosity mehsoos hui thi. Harish–Sunil ka changing-room incident abhi bhi uski knowledge se bahar tha.

## M07 — The Senior Consultation

10:20–10:45 AM · Hospital consultation area · Draft for review

10:20 AM. Dr. Ritesh Anand ke consultation room mein Meenakshi apne notes ke saath aayi. Dono kaam mein purane colleagues the; Ritesh ko Meenakshi ka direct answer dene ka andaaz achhi tarah pata tha, aur Meenakshi jaanti thi ki woh half-prepared case ko kabhi lightly nahi leta.

Ritesh: “Tumne repeat investigation ka trend bheja tha. Diagnosis se pehle ek missing baseline clear karna hai.”

Meenakshi: “Maine previous readings attach ki hain. Last value confusing hai, isliye single report par decision nahi liya.”

Agale pandrah minute pure clinical discussion mein nikle. Ritesh ne ek assumption challenge ki; Meenakshi ne data dikhakar apna reasoning defend kiya. Unhone revised consultation note agree kiya. Yahan koi flirting nahi thi—Meenakshi ne case lead kiya aur Ritesh ne uske judgment ko seriously liya.

10:37 AM. Patient file close ho gayi. Ritesh ne coffee cup uthaya aur Meenakshi ki taraf dekha. Standing desk par Meenakshi thodi jhuki hui file sign kar rahi thi. White coat ka front halka khula tha; fabric uski waist par ek crease banata hua fitted top ki outline dikha raha tha. Ritesh ki nazar uske chehre se coat ki crease tak gayi, phir wapas uski aankhon par aa thahri.

Ritesh: “Ek baat kahun? Tum aaj kal apni meetings mein kaafi dangerous confidence lekar aati ho.”

Meenakshi: “Dangerous report mein likha karo, sir. Compliment dena hai toh clear bolo.”

Ritesh: “Theek hai. White coat tum par achha fit hota hai. Aur tumhe pata hai kab saamne wale ki nazar tum par hai.”

Meenakshi ka pen ek pal ko ruk gaya. Usne posture seedha kiya, coat ke front ko halka sa adjust kiya aur seedhe Ritesh ki aankhon mein dekha. Ritesh ki line Avi ke teasing se alag thi—na loud, na playful excuse. Meenakshi ko apne chehre par aati muskaan ka ehsaas tha, lekin usne use chhupaya nahi.

Meenakshi: “Main doctor hoon. Patient observe karti hoon. Lekin aaj lag raha hai department mein observers kuch zyada hain.”

Ritesh: “Toh main bhi waiting list mein hoon?”

Meenakshi: “Depends. Consultation chahiye ya bas mujhe dobara dekhne ka bahana?”

Ritesh ne coffee cup table par rakha aur thoda aage jhuka. Meenakshi ne file apne seene ke kareeb kheench li, lekin peeche nahi hati. Yeh koi clinical discussion nahi reh gaya tha; phir bhi usne apni smile aur jawab dono par control rakha.

Ritesh: “Aapki diagnosis se bachna mushkil hai, Dr Kapoor.”

Meenakshi: “Aur meri permission ke bina follow-up book karna bhi.”

Ritesh hansa. Meenakshi bhi muskuraayi. Unke beech purani familiarity thi, lekin senior-junior designation ka bhi weight tha. Usne isliye joke ko wahi rok diya; na koi personal promise diya, na kaam ke decisions ko usse mix hone diya.

Ritesh: “Tumhari sabse risky habit pata hai? Tum kisi ko ek line se chup kara sakti ho, phir aise smile karti ho jaise kuch hua hi nahi.”

Meenakshi: “Aur aapki? Aap clinical review khatam karke bhi unnecessary observations note karte rehte ho.”

Ritesh: “Professional weakness maan leta hoon.”

Ritesh: “Weakness yeh hai ki clinical review khatam hone ke baad bhi nazar file par nahi, aap par hi rehti hai. Aur aapko pata bhi hai.”

Meenakshi: “Weakness accepted. Bas medical record mein mat daal dena.”

10:44 AM. Meenakshi ne consultation file uthayi. Door tak pahunchte hue use ehsaas hua jaise Ritesh ki nazar abhi bhi uski peeth par ho. Usne palat kar nahi dekha. Uski dhadkan ek pal ko tez hui—use khud bhi hairaani thi ki woh is acknowledgement ko itni der yaad rakhegi. Phir usne handle pakda aur apni marzi se conversation wahi khatam kar di.

10:45 AM. Meenakshi senior review ki preparation karne chali gayi. Agla official scene 11:00 AM ka M08 — Senior Review tha. M07 ke flirtation se koi private agreement, physical advance ya locker access justify nahi hota.
