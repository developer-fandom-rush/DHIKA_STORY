## Ankita EP03 — Scene 03, Beat 1: Private Briefing

**1 January 2026 · 14:30–14:45 · Cyber City Office**

Bhalla aur Sandeep ne Ankita ko briefing room mein bulaya. Door band hua. Sandeep ne kaha ki final sign-off discussion private rakhni hai. Table pe release documents the, lekin unki nazar documents pe kam thi.

Ankita ne file table par rakhi. Beige blazer ke neeche cream embroidered kurti ka fabric uske baithne aur dobara khade hone se halka sa shift hua. Use sabki attention ka ehsaas hua.

**Bhalla:** “Ankita, aaj tumhara look kaafi sharp hai. Kurti mein confidence clearly dikh raha hai.”

Uski awaaz calm thi, lekin nazar Ankita ke neckline se waist tak gayi. Ankita ne woh nazar notice ki. Bhalla ne bina hichkichahat apni aankhein wapas uske chehre par tikayi.

**Sandeep:** “Haan. Subah se log notice kar rahe hain. Figure mein jo change aaya hai, woh ignore karna mushkil hai.”

Ankita ko subah ki teasing yaad aa gayi. Aaj ki baar-baar ki tareef uske mann mein reh gayi thi.

Ankita ne unki taraf dekha. Woh seedha khadi thi, shoulders back.

**Ankita:** “Aap dono release review kar rahe ho ya meri appearance ka audit?”

**Bhalla:** “Dono. Results impressive hain… dono jagah.”

**Ankita:** “Toh report mein clearly mention kariyega. Half observations mujhe bilkul pasand nahi.”

**Sandeep:** “Careful, Ankita. Itni detailed review maangogi toh meeting lambi ho sakti hai.”

**Ankita:** “Meeting ki length se problem nahi hai, Sandeep. Bas reviewers capable hone chahiye.”

Ek chhota sa pause aaya. Sandeep ne jawab dene se pehle Ankita ka expression padha. Woh muskura rahi thi, lekin nazar nahi hata rahi thi.

**Bhalla:** “I think Sandeep just underestimated the person he was evaluating.”

**Sandeep:** “Especially jab tum move karti ho. Kurti ka fit har step pe clear hota hai.”

Ankita ne table ke kinare haath rakha. Uska upper body thoda aage jhuka. Kurti ki neckline ke paas fabric halka sa muda, aur collarbone dikha.

Woh kuch pal wahi ruki, dono ki reactions dekhi, phir dheere se seedhi hui.

**Ankita:** “Toh aap logon ko lagta hai main jaan-bujh ke attention le rahi hoon?”

**Bhalla:** “Jaan-bujh ke ya naturally… farq nahi padta. Asar same hai.”

**Ankita:** “Interesting. Lekin attention dena aur attention handle karna, dono alag skills hain. Hai na?”

Sandeep ne apni tie thodi adjust ki. Uski nazar Ankita ke hips pe ek pal ko ruki.

**Sandeep:** “Aur asar kaafi strong hai.”

Ankita ne apni fingers table se hata li. Uske chehre par smile thi, lekin andar ek alag si warmth ubhar rahi thi—tareef sunne ki khushi, thodi nervous curiosity aur ye jaanne ki ichchha ki dono asal mein kitne sincere hain.

**Ankita:** “Aap logon ki observations toh kaafi detailed hain. Kabhi-kabhi lagta hai main meeting lead kar rahi hoon, aur focus kahin aur chal raha hai.”

**Bhalla:** “Focus toh aap hi hain, Ankita. Bas discussion ka subject badalta ja raha hai.”

**Ankita:** “Toh phir subject badalne ki permission bhi mujhse leni padegi.”

Bhalla ne halka sa sir jhukaya.

**Bhalla:** “Fair enough. Aaj room mein final authority aapki.”

Ankita ko apni heartbeat phir mehsoos hui. Is baar woh usse chhupane ke bajay apni breathing steady karne lagi. Use achha lag raha tha ki conversation ka control uske paas hai, lekin ye bhi pata tha ki woh khud apni curiosity se surprised hai.

**Sandeep:** “Waise Ankita, mujhe lagta hai tumhe achhi tarah pata hai log tumhe notice karte hain.”

**Ankita:** “Pata hona aur confirm karna alag baat hai.”

**Sandeep:** “Aur confirmation milne ke baad?”

**Ankita:** “Depends. Result kitna convincing hai.”

Ek pal ke liye teeno ki nazrein miliं. Sandeep ne apni tie dobara seedhi ki; Bhalla ki measured smile ab bhi bani hui thi.

Ankita ne room ke glass panel mein apna reflection dekha. Uska blazer, neatly fitted kurti aur composed posture wahi the jo subah se the. Badla tha toh uska unhe dekhne ka tareeka.

Room mein tension dheere-dheere badh rahi thi. Koi touch nahi hua. Sirf nazar aur words the.

**Ankita (thinking):** “Subah se sab kuch na kuch notice kar rahe hain. Shayad mujhe bhi dekhna chahiye ki inki tareef mein kitni sachchai hai.”
